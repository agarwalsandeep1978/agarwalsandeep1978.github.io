Japalouppe Equestrian Centre

Two residential programmes combining horse riding, outdoor learning and responsibility.

The experience

Varish completed two 10-day residential programmes at Japalouppe Equestrian Centre near Pune in 2021, covering riding lessons and sessions on horse breeds, grooming and behaviour, along with survival and nature-based learning.

Progression

On the second visit, Varish completed Level II and developed horse-riding and horse-management skills including grooming, stable management and horse tack.

Personal takeaway

The experience shaped character, channelled empathy and built a lasting sense of responsibility for another living being.
