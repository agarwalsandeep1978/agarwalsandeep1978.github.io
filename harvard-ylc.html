Leadership · Social impact

Harvard Youth Lead the Change

A five-day leadership programme in Mumbai that combined self-reflection with practical work on communication, collaboration, problem-mapping and social change.

3–7 January 2024 · B.D. Somani International School, Mumbai

Read my programme reflections (PDF)View programme brochure

What the programme covered

The programme introduced leadership styles and servant leadership, then moved into teamwork, design thinking, negotiation, conflict resolution, presentations and project planning. The brochure describes students working in teams to understand a global issue and develop a social-change project.

My starting point

In my application, I wrote about wanting to understand leadership beyond the conventional business setting—particularly how it could be applied to social, climate and cultural issues. I was interested in how economic and cultural differences shape leadership, and in learning how to turn concern about a problem into action.

Project Heal

My leadership-programme work centred on Project Heal: a social-impact concept focused on hygiene awareness in urban communities, using collaboration between corporates and students and an incentive-based approach. The key question for me was how to make participation in a social initiative easier to sustain.
