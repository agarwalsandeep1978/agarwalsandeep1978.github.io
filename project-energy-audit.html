Maker Profile · Project 03

Home Energy Audit Initiative

I turned professional exposure to energy auditing into a practical sustainability initiative in my residential community.

From observation to a model

After noticing that energy conservation was rarely discussed systematically in middle-class homes, I shadowed an energy-audit consultant at SENERGY. I then developed a residential audit model using Excel to estimate appliance-level costs from wattage, usage hours and tariffs.

Auditing homes

I interviewed residents, identified consumption patterns and energy-saving opportunities, and audited 28 apartments. I shared recommendations designed to reduce consumption without changing residents’ lifestyles and followed up after three months. Homes that implemented the recommendations reduced their electricity bills by approximately 36%.

Extending the impact

The exercise had significant community impact, and I am exploring how to formalise the model into a portal where students could use my research and audit framework to assess flats in their own societies, extending its potential impact to a much larger community.
