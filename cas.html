Adventure, challenge & growth

Summer 2026: kitesurfing, Discover Scuba Diving and tandem skydiving.

Kitesurfing

Two structured sessions progressed from kite control and body-dragging toward a first water start. The biggest lesson was perseverance: repeated crashes became data rather than failure.

Discover Scuba

Returning after a three-year gap, Varish rebuilt confidence through supervised descents, buoyancy practice and careful attention to safety. Seeing a living reef ecosystem strengthened appreciation for marine conservation.

Tandem Skydiving

The challenge was primarily psychological: managing anticipation, trusting preparation and committing despite fear. The experience reinforced preparation, safety protocols and trusting skilled professionals.

CAS learning

Together these experiences strongly evidence learning around strengths and growth, new challenges and skills, initiating and planning, commitment and perseverance, and safety/ethical choices.
