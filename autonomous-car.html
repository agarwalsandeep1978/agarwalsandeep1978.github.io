Maker Profile · Grade 10 Design

Autonomous Car

I explored autonomous movement and obstacle avoidance through a design project that combined sensors, electronics, motor control and iterative testing. My initial design thinking was shaped by a real-world problem: large amounts of waste on Mumbai’s beaches and the limits of relying only on manual clean-ups.

Designing the system

My design work investigated how an autonomous vehicle could sense its surroundings and respond safely. In the circuit plan, I used an ESP32 as the controller, an L298N dual H-bridge motor driver to operate four motors, an ultrasonic sensor to detect obstacles and a gyroscope to monitor orientation. The ultrasonic sensor was intended to measure the distance ahead so the vehicle could stop rather than collide with an object. The gyroscope was included to help monitor the vehicle’s orientation as it moved.

I first developed the circuit in Tinkercad and planned the control logic around reading sensor data, interpreting distance and orientation, and sending the appropriate signals to the motor driver. This helped me think of the car as an integrated system: sensing, decision-making and movement had to work together rather than operate as isolated components.

Building and evaluating

The making process involved assembling the chassis, securing the motors and their connectors, wiring the electronic components and developing code for sensor readings and motor control. I had to consider practical issues such as stable motor mounting, correct pin connections, reliable sensor readings and how the vehicle would behave when an obstacle was detected. My design reports document the circuit, assembly tasks and evaluation against the criteria established during planning.

The project taught me that an autonomous system depends on more than a working circuit. The physical build, the sensor data and the control logic all influence whether the vehicle responds as intended. It also strengthened my interest in robotics as a way to apply engineering to environmental problems.
