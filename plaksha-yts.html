Young Technology Scholars, Plaksha University

A two-week residential programme exploring engineering through interdisciplinary lectures, programming and electronics exercises, hands-on projects and mentorship.

9–23 June 2024 · Plaksha University, Mohali

Read my application reflections (PDF)View programme brochure

Learning through building

The YTS curriculum connected fundamentals of computation, hardware interfaces, programming and microcontrollers with real-world challenges. The programme’s maker-builder approach was a good fit for my interest in understanding how mathematics and physics become working systems.

Teleoperation project

During the programme, I worked on a teleoperation project and gained hands-on experience building a working robot model. The experience helped connect technical concepts with the practical decisions involved in making a system operate.

What I wanted to explore

In my application, I described a keen interest in engineering and in applying mathematics and science to improve quality of life. I wanted to understand different engineering streams and see how technology extends beyond digital tools or automation into a range of real-world applications.
